Global tensions remain high as major events unfold across the Middle East and Africa, alongside ongoing European conflict. In Iran, the situation is marked by severe economic hardship and escalating conflict, fueled by internal power struggles indicated by reports of generals taking control, and external threats including U.S. demands regarding maritime security in the Strait of Hormuz. Simultaneously, the international focus is on the protracted conflict in Ukraine, reflected by the EU's assessment that peace is distant, which is tied to a substantial $106 billion loan. Meanwhile, instability is evident in Tanzania, where an inquiry has documented the killing of over 500 people following election violence.
